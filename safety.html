Trust & Safety

Your security is our absolute priority, built directly into the fabric of the app.

At Trulo, we believe dating should be exciting, natural, and above all, deeply safe. The online dating space has historically failed to adequately protect its users from fraud, harassment, and physical danger. We are taking a radically different approach.

Mandatory ID Verification

Every single person on Trulo has to pass a secure, third-party ID check. We verify that they are a real person and that the age on their profile strictly matches their government ID. If they don't pass, they don't get an account. Simple as that.

Personal Audit Trail

Meeting a stranger from the internet carries inherent risk. To mitigate this, Trulo includes a secure Audit Trail feature. Before your date, tell the app where you're going and who you are meeting. This creates a secure offline ledger that gives you an added layer of peace of mind.

Zero Tolerance Policy

Trulo does not tolerate abuse, harassment, fraud, or inappropriate behaviour. Because our platform requires ID verification, getting banned from Trulo means being banned permanently. It is much harder for bad actors to simply spin up a new account.

Data Privacy

We use your ID solely to verify your authenticity. This sensitive physical identity data is processed securely by our verification partners and is strictly protected. We will never sell your personal data or your dating history. For full details on how we handle your personal information, please read our Privacy Policy.

Report and Block

If someone makes you feel uncomfortable, behaves inappropriately, or violates our terms of service, you can easily block and report them directly from their profile or your chat window. Our Trust & Safety team reviews all reports swiftly to ensure the community remains safe.
