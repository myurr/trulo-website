Our Story

Designed differently.

We fixed the things you hate about dating apps.

Verified Users Only

Every user goes through a mandatory ID check. Know confidently that they're a real person, and that their age is correct.

Travel Time Search

Don't search by a useless crow-flies radius. Search for people by actual travel time. "I want to meet someone within 1 hour of me".

Customizable Profiles

Show off the real you. Fully customizable profiles based on flexible content blocks that let your personality shine.

Focus on the Date

Limited parallel conversations and limited messages. We encourage you to agree to a date or exchange numbers, rather than finding pen pals.

Personal Audit Trail

Tell the app the details of the dates you go on. We create a secure audit trail to protect your personal security.

Curated Date Ideas

Need inspiration? The app will suggest date ideas for you — places near him, near her, or somewhere perfectly in the middle.
