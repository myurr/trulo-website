Cookies & Third-Party Resources

Last updated: February 2026. Plain English: what loads when you visit trulo.dating.

The short version

trulo.dating does not set any cookies itself. We don't run analytics, advertising pixels, session tracking, or session replay. There is no cookie consent banner because there is nothing to consent to on our side.

However, this website does load two resources from third parties — used only to render the page or to handle contact form submissions. Each is documented below.

1. Google Fonts (font rendering)

Pages on this site use the Inter typeface, served by Google Fonts via fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. When your browser fetches the font, Google receives your IP address and User-Agent string as part of the standard HTTP request.

  • Google Fonts does not set cookies on this domain.
  • Google Fonts does not track users between websites for advertising purposes — it is a CDN service.
  • Google's stated retention for Fonts API logs is short-term diagnostic only.
  • Google's privacy policy applies to data received: policies.google.com/privacy.

2. Formspree (contact form)

The contact form on our contact page submits to Formspree, a form-handling service. We use Formspree so that the messages you send us reach our inbox without exposing personal email accounts to spam. Formspree only receives the data you choose to put in the form (your name, email, subject, and message).

  • Formspree may set cookies on its own domain (formspree.io) to prevent automated abuse of the form. These are first-party to Formspree, not to trulo.dating.
  • If you don't submit the contact form, no data is sent to Formspree.
  • Formspree's privacy policy: formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

3. Local storage

We do not use localStorage, sessionStorage, or any browser-side storage on this website. If you join the waitlist, only what you choose to submit is stored — and only on our servers, in the United Kingdom / European Economic Area.

4. The Trulo mobile app

This cookie policy covers the website at trulo.dating only. The Trulo mobile app uses no advertising or analytics SDKs — its data handling is described in full in our Privacy Policy.

5. Changes to this policy

If we ever add cookies or trackers to trulo.dating, we will update this page first, add a consent banner if required by UK PECR / GDPR, and post the change date at the top.

Questions? Email privacy@trulo.dating.
